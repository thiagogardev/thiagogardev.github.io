# Software engineering and applied AI for industry

Executive director of industrial companies (manufacturing of quick couplings, import of mining supplies) and production engineer. Graduate student in **Cognitive Systems with AI Agents**.

This is the index of what I built to run my own companies: software that replaced paid systems, automated B2B prospecting and survived the total loss of the company's physical servers. Everything is in production, used every day, with a real problem on the other side.

**Principles shared by every project**

- Open source and self-hosted: nothing depends on a managed platform. Postgres, Docker and nginx/Caddy on a VPS I operate.
- Business data stays out of the code. Keys live only in `.env` files, never versioned. Secret scanning before any push.
- Human in the loop: the agents prepare, prioritize and suggest; the commercial decision and the contact are human.
- Documentation as the source of truth: every system has architecture, database, deployment and restore-runbook docs.
