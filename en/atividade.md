# How I work with AI agents

Almost everything in this portfolio was built and is operated with AI coding agents (Claude Code), across several sessions a day. Each session takes a request, drafts a step-by-step plan, executes it, verifies the result and closes with a summary of what is done and what is still open. This page shows the log of those closings, numbers only.

{% include atividade.html %}

## What is counted

- **Task closed:** a request the session finished and summarized, stating what changed and what was not verified yet. Requests dropped halfway do not count.
- **Commit:** commits made that day in the systems' repositories (all private, except this portfolio).
- **Production deploy:** releases to the VPS where the systems run, grouped per session.
- **Area:** each task is classified by the subject of its own summary: systems and ERPs, infrastructure and security, company administration, marketing and websites, career and studies.

## What stays out

No task text, session title, customer, amount or infrastructure detail leaves my computer. The full dashboard, with the daily feed, is private. Only a file with daily counts comes here, generated by a script and published by a scheduled job.

## Why publish this

Because it shows the method better than a list of tools: a human in the loop, a plan before execution, verification before closing and a record of what was done. The [Genesis](cases/genesis.md), [Clinalta](cases/clinalta.md), [Escrituro](cases/escrituro.md) and [ICMS ST Monitor](cases/icms-st.md) case studies show the outcome of this method as products.
